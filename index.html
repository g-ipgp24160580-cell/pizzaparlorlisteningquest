Pizzeria Listening Quest

Year 4 ESL English Listening Comprehension Challenge

How to Play:

1. Listen: Walk to the customer and listen carefully to their English pizza order.

2. Knead & Prep: Go to the Prep Counter to prepare dough and spread sauce.

3. Toppings: Choose exact ingredients requested by the customer.

4. Bake: Place pizza in the brick wood oven.

5. Serve & Quiz: Deliver to customer and answer English listening comprehension questions!

Controls: WASD / Arrow Keys to walk | Mouse Drag to look | Left-Click / E Key to interact.
Mobile/Tablet: On-screen joystick, drag screen to rotate camera, tap button to interact.
